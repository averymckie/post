**What the document is.** It's a spec for a three-level factory. A family generator takes a formal product-family definition and emits a configurator. At runtime there is no language model anywhere. Every transformation is a named public library callable at a pinned version, wired into a chain with typed handoffs. The model's only job is upstream and at rest: read sources, propose interpretations, pick libraries, design compositions. Once accepted, those become versioned assets and the model is gone.

**The load-bearing rules.** Every edge between two functions must satisfy a fourteen-field contract, not just a matching schema: meaning, identity, time, evidence basis, coverage, authority, state, effects, numerics, resources, evidence, change and exceptions. A missing adapter blocks the edge. A purpose label is not an implementation. Evidence basis never gets promoted silently, so a mention is not an event and a scenario is not history. Acceptance is a conjunction over every leaf, edge, composition, environment and global obligation, never an average. Proof is by structural induction over a finite construction tree, and the induction is conditional on the leaves and bindings actually being discharged. ### M3-04 · HL7 FHIR conformance rules · FORD 3 / ISIC Q
**Beneficiary, problem, context.** Patients whose records must move between a hospital, a clinic and an app, and the implementers who must be able to say precisely what their system does and does not support. "We support FHIR" is not a testable claim unless conformance itself is specified.
**Source.** HL7 International — *FHIR*, github.com/HL7/fhir, `source/conformance-rules.html` (lines 26–31, 41–44, 53, 71, 77–78, 84–88); read via raw.githubusercontent.com. **READ.**
**Deliverables required.** A conformance claim scoped to a specific exchange mechanism, optionally published as a CapabilityStatement. **Inputs.** Implementation capabilities; profiles; the conformance-layer resources.
**Requirements.**
- `M3-04/R1` **[S]** Applications claiming conformance make the claim **in respect of a specific** exchange mechanism, and claim conformance to **one (or more)** of the listed exchange paradigms — conformance is scoped, never global.
- `M3-04/R2` **[S]** FHIR provides a **conformance layer** implemented using named conformance resources.
- `M3-04/R3` **[S]** Systems wishing to assert formal conformance **MAY** publish a **CapabilityStatement** defining how they conform; assertions of conformance in the absence of one are of limited value.
- `M3-04/R4` **[S]** The specification uses **SHALL, SHOULD, MAY** per RFC 8174's clarification of RFC 2119, but — **unlike RFC 2119** — it allows that different (contextual) readings apply.
- `M3-04/R5` **[S]** **SHALL** = an absolute requirement for all implementations; **SHALL NOT** = an absolute prohibition; **SHOULD/SHOULD NOT** = best practice to be considered, which may be ignored for valid reasons in a particular implementation context.
- `M3-04/R6` **[S]** "FHIR-like" solutions (based on FHIR but not conformant) set trading-partner expectations that are then unmet — a stated hazard, not merely a preference.
**Component relationships.** CapabilityStatement → implementation behaviour (the machine-readable claim). Profile → resource constraints (narrowing for a use case). Conformance resources → automated testing tools. Trading partner A ↔ B (expectations formed from claims; R6 is about what happens when claims are loose).
**Whole-product constraints.** Conformance is *plural and scoped*: two conformant systems may not interoperate, which is the specification's own acknowledged condition. SHOULD is deliberately escapable with reasons, so testable conformance rests on SHALL plus the published CapabilityStatement. Clinical data carries safety consequences, so silent partial support is dangerous.
**Acceptance.** AC1 *(source)*: a conformance claim names the exchange mechanism(s) it applies to. AC2 *(source)*: a published CapabilityStatement accurately describes supported resources and interactions. AC3 *(proposed — requires acceptance)*: an automated test derived from the CapabilityStatement passes against the live system. AC4 *(proposed — requires acceptance)*: any SHOULD not implemented is listed with its reason.
**Assumptions, gaps, unresolved.** **[I]** R4's allowance for contextual readings of the key words is unusual and weakens mechanical conformance checking. I read the conformance-rules page only, not the full specification.

### S5-01 · Psych-DS · FORD 5 / ISIC M
**Beneficiary, problem, context.** Psychology researchers and the people who later reuse their data — meta-analysts, replicators, reviewers. **Requirements.**

### S5-05 · OSF preregistration · FORD 5 / ISIC M · **SNIPPET — unverified**

### H6-04 · Erlangen CRM / CIDOC CRM in OWL · FORD 6 / ISIC R
**Beneficiary, problem, context.** Museums, archives and heritage researchers who need to state that *this object was made by that person at that place in that period, according to this scholarly opinion* — and to reconcile that statement with another institution's contradictory one. **Source.** FAU Erlangen (Erlangen CRM) implementing CIDOC CRM — github.com/erlangen-crm/ecrm, `README.md`: `ecrm_current.owl` (current version, namespace `http://erlangen-crm.org/current/`, **includes `rdfs:label` for each class and property**); `ecrm_&lt;yymmdd&gt;.owl` (older versions, namespace `http://erlangen-crm.org/&lt;yymmdd&gt;/`). **Deliverables required.** A versioned OWL-DL ontology implementing CIDOC CRM, with labelled classes and properties, at dated namespaces. **Inputs.** The CIDOC CRM specification; OWL-DL expressivity constraints.
**Requirements.**
- `H6-04/R1` **[S]** Each release is published at a **date-stamped namespace** (`/yymmdd/`), with `/current/` as the moving pointer — **published data pins a date, not `current`**.
- `H6-04/R2` **[S]** The current version **includes `rdfs:label` for every class and property**; older versions do not necessarily.
- `H6-04/R3` **[S]** The artefact is an **OWL** implementation, so it is bound by OWL-DL expressivity — some CRM statements are representable only approximately.
- `H6-04/R4` **[I]** Conformance is two-layered: to CIDOC CRM (the conceptual model) and to this OWL encoding of it.
- `H6-04/R5` **[S-unverified, from CIDOC CRM]** CIDOC CRM is an ISO standard (ISO 21127:2023) — cited from search results, not read.
**Component relationships.** CIDOC CRM specification → Erlangen OWL encoding (implementation, with loss). Dated namespace → published triples (identity across time; changing the namespace changes every URI). Labels → human-facing tools. Event-centric model → objects, actors, places, times (the CRM's core move: everything is asserted through events, which is what allows contradictory attributions to coexist).

**Acceptance.** AC1 *(source)*: every class and property in `ecrm_current.owl` has an `rdfs:label`. AC2 *(source)*: the ontology loads as valid OWL-DL. AC3 *(source)*: a dated release remains resolvable after `current` advances. AC4 *(proposed — requires acceptance)*: two contradictory attributions of the same object coexist without inconsistency, each traceable to its asserter.
**Assumptions, gaps, unresolved.** **[I]** Which CIDOC CRM version `ecrm_current` implements was not established from the README. The base standard is behind a host I could not reach — **a real gap for any conformance claim**.

**T-01 ILO C189** — (i) **Household-scale terms-of-employment generator**: produces a plain-language written statement covering R1's required particulars, in the worker's language and the employer's, for a household that has never employed anyone. (ii) **Stand-by hours recorder**: a simple, low-friction record of stand-by periods (R6) that both parties can see, since the definitional ambiguity of stand-by is where pay disputes originate.

**U-01 IATI** — (i) **Funding-chain tracer**: reconstructs donor→implementer→sub-implementer chains from activity identifiers (my AC4) and reports where the chain breaks. (ii) **Ruleset triage report**: since publishers are unfunded, a report that ranks a publisher's ruleset failures by how much they impede downstream use, so limited effort goes where it matters.

**U-04 Open SDG** — (i) **Zero-target honest renderer**: displays elimination targets with an explicit note that 0.001 is a substitute (my AC4), fixing a genuine misrepresentation. (ii) **Proxy prominence audit**: reports how visible proxy flags (R3) are in the rendered site, since a caveat buried below a chart does not function as a caveat.

**N1-04 VOTable** — (i) **Self-contained interpretability check**: verifies a document remains interpretable with the vocabulary service unreachable (my AC5). (ii) **Dangling-ref linter**: enforces R6 (referenced `TIMESYS` must exist) across an archive, catching a failure that silently produces wrong times.

**N1-02 OPTIMADE** — (i) **Provenance-preserving federated result set**: presents results such that each entry's provider is inseparable from it (my AC5), preventing silent conflation. (ii) **Cross-provider duplicate reporter**: identifies structures that appear in several providers under different `(type,id)` pairs — the limitation provider-scoped identity creates.

**E2-05 RISC-V SBI** — (i) **Reserved-bits fuzzer**: probes firmware with non-zero reserved bits to determine reject-versus-ignore behaviour (the ambiguity), publishing a compatibility matrix across implementations. (ii) **Boot-order violation harness**: issues runtime calls before boot-time setup to verify a defined error rather than undefined behaviour (my AC5).

**E2-03 riscv-dv** — (i) **Seed-replay proof**: verifies a recorded seed reproduces an identical program bit-for-bit (my AC3), which the whole debugging workflow assumes. (ii) **Mismatch attribution assistant**: presents the first divergent instruction with architectural state from both DUT and reference (my AC4), addressing "which side is wrong".

**M3-03 PHA4GE** — (i) **Template version crosswalk**: publishes machine-readable upgrades between specification versions (my AC4), so mid-outbreak vocabulary changes do not strand submitted data. (ii) **Re-identification risk screen**: estimates, from sewershed population, when a sample's location becomes identifying — the unresolved threshold, made a check.

**M3-04 HL7 FHIR** — (i) **CapabilityStatement-derived test suite**: generates conformance tests directly from a system's published statement (my AC3), turning a claim into evidence. (ii) **SHOULD-exception register**: a published list of unimplemented SHOULDs with reasons (my AC4), so trading partners can see the shape of partial support that R6 warns about.

**A4-05 Open Foris Arena** — (i) **Cycle-to-cycle variable mapping**: documents how a revised survey's variables map to the previous cycle (my AC4), preserving the time series that is the whole value of a national inventory. (ii) **Reproducibility receipt**: re-runs a processing chain and certifies byte-identical statistics (my AC3), which official outputs require.

**A4-04 FoodOn** — (i) **Deprecation redirect service**: resolves retired terms to replacements (my AC4), protecting data annotated years earlier. (ii) **Cultural variance annotator**: represents competing culturally-specific definitions of one food side by side rather than forcing a single winner — the unresolved modelling question.

**S5-01 Psych-DS** — (i) **Submission-gate validator**: a journal- or repository-side check that a deposited dataset passes `columnsMatchMetadata` and naming rules before acceptance (R2, R3). (ii) **Undocumented-column nudger**: an authoring-time assistant that prompts for a `variableMeasured` entry the moment a new column appears, aligning the behavioural demand with the moment of work.

**S5-05 OSF preregistration** — (i) **Amendment-preserving deviation log**: records deviations as amendments pointing to the original (my AC4), so honest deviation is easier than silent revision. (ii) **Ordering evidence attachment**: attaches independently time-stamped evidence that data collection had not begun — addressing the central unresolved limitation, that the artefact cannot prove its own timing.

**H6-04 Erlangen CRM** — (i) **Contradiction-tolerant attribution viewer**: displays competing attributions of one object with their asserters (my AC4), which is what the event-centric model exists to permit but tools rarely show. (ii) **Namespace-pinning linter**: flags published data that references `/current/` instead of a dated namespace (R1), preventing silent meaning drift.

**H6-01 TEI P5** — (i) **Customisation conformance card**: states which modules and constraints a project's ODD selects, so "TEI-conformant" becomes a specific claim (R2, R4). (ii) **Uncertainty survival test**: verifies that `CE-` certainty and responsibility encodings survive a publication pipeline rather than being flattened (my AC4).

### 4.2 Composed systems (recursive composition; each explains what passes between parts and what must stay consistent)

**CS-1 · Field-to-payment assurance chain** — `A-01` fiboa boundaries + `A-02` Modus soil results + `A4-03` ADAPT operations + `G-05` UNTP DPP. *What passes:* field identity `(collection,id)` from fiboa; soil analyte values with method identity from Modus; application records from ADAPT; all of it becomes evidence (`G-05/R6`) behind a claim in a product passport. *What must stay consistent:* the field identifier across all four, and the time basis — fiboa's observation-time semantics (`A-01/R6`) must not be conflated with operation time. *Emergent outcome:* a claim like "grown on land not deforested after date X with nutrient application within limits" that carries verifiable evidence rather than assertion. *Inherited requirements:* fiboa R1–R7, Modus R2 (method governance), UNTP R1 (granularity) and R7 (VCDM 2.0).

**CS-2 · Repair evidence loop** — `S-01` ORDS + `S-04` iFixit rubric + `G-05` DPP + `E-04`-style profile thinking. *What passes:* repair attempt outcomes by product category (ORDS codelists) upward into repairability evidence; scores downward into passports at model granularity (`G-05/R1`). *What must stay consistent:* product category identity across ORDS codelists and passport product identity; rubric version stamping (`S-04/R7`). *Emergent outcome:* a repairability claim on a product passport grounded in what actually happened at repair cafés, not only in a lab teardown. *Note:* ORDS and iFixit share an origin cluster — this composition therefore does not constitute independent corroboration.

**CS-3 · Outbreak-to-plate traceback** — `I-05` FSMA 204 + `G-01` EPCIS + `M3-03` PHA4GE + `A4-04` FoodOn. *What passes:* traceability lot codes and CTE/KDE records (FSMA) expressed as EPCIS events; wastewater genomic signals (PHA4GE) as the detection trigger; FoodOn terms reconciling product descriptions across parties. *What must stay consistent:* lot identity through transformation (`I-05/R4`) and food-term identity (FoodOn ontology IDs) across supply-chain participants. *Emergent outcome:* the 24-hour spreadsheet obligation (`I-05/R5`) becomes a query rather than a fire drill. *Feasibility caveat:* FSMA requirements are unverified; this composition should be treated as a design hypothesis.

**CS-4 · Building lifecycle handover** — `F-01` BCF + `F-02` BuildingSync + `F-04` Brick + `F-05` RealEstateCore. *What passes:* design-stage issues and their viewpoints (BCF) resolve into as-built element identities; audit findings (BuildingSync, at a declared level) attach to those elements; Brick/REC carry them into operation. *What must stay consistent:* element identity across model revisions — the unresolved question in `F-01` — and audit level declarations. *Emergent outcome:* an operator can ask "which unresolved design issue explains this HVAC fault?" *Inherited:* `F-01/R1` field consistency; `F-02/R2` per-use-case conformance.

**CS-5 · Mobility trip across operators** — `H-01` GTFS + `H-02` GBFS + `H-03` TOMP + `H-06` MDS. *What passes:* scheduled service (GTFS), vehicle availability (GBFS), booking and payment (TOMP), and regulatory reporting (MDS). *What must stay consistent:* stop/place identity between GTFS and GBFS station definitions; a trip identifier that survives the operator boundary. *Emergent outcome:* a single journey combining bus and shared bike that a city can also regulate. *Cluster note:* GTFS and GBFS share MobilityData stewardship.

**CS-6 · Clinical study definition to evidence** — `M3-01` CDISC USDM + `M3-04` FHIR + `Q-02` OMOP + `S5-05` preregistration. *What passes:* a machine-readable study definition (USDM) drives data capture (FHIR) which lands in a common model (OMOP) for analysis; the analysis plan is frozen beforehand (preregistration). *What must stay consistent:* endpoint definitions across all four, and the frozen analysis plan against the executed one. *Emergent outcome:* deviation between planned and executed analysis becomes detectable rather than deniable. *Inherited:* `Q-02/R2` ETL conventions; `M3-04/R1` scoped conformance.

**CS-7 · Public money to public outcome** — `O-02` OCDS + `U-01` IATI + `S-03` 360Giving + `U-04` Open SDG. *What passes:* contracting processes (`ocid`), aid activities (IATI identifiers), grants (360Giving), aggregated into indicator movement (Open SDG). *What must stay consistent:* organisation identity across four schemes — the hardest part — and immutability of published records (`O-02/R1`). *Emergent outcome:* tracing a stated SDG indicator change back to the contracts and grants that plausibly produced it.

**CS-8 · Heritage object across institutions** — `H6-04` CIDOC/Erlangen CRM + `R-03` Linked Art + `R-01` IIIF + `H6-01` TEI + `H6-02` RiC-O. *What passes:* object and event identity (CRM), presentation of images (IIIF), transcription of associated texts (TEI), and archival context of the records (RiC-O). *What must stay consistent:* the dated CRM namespace (`H6-04/R1`) across institutions, and the identity of the object across all representations. *Emergent outcome:* a contested attribution can be shown with its evidence — image, transcript, archival record — and its asserter.

**CS-9 · Energy at the meter boundary** — `D-01` OCPI + `D-03` OCPP + `D-04` EEBus + `D-02` SunSpec. *What passes:* the charger-to-operator link (OCPP), the operator-to-provider roaming link (OCPI), the in-home negotiation (EEBus), and inverter/battery state (SunSpec). *What must stay consistent:* EVSE identity across OCPP and OCPI — the seam noted in `D-01` — and the physical limit that all parties are negotiating against. *Emergent outcome:* a car charging on a visiting network without exceeding the house fuse, billed correctly.

**CS-10 · Survey instrument to public statistic** — `M-01` ODK XForms + `S5-04` XLSForm + `A4-05` Arena + `S5-03` DDI-CDI + `S5-02` SDMX + `U-05` NADA. *What passes:* an authored form (XLSForm→XForms), field collection (Arena/ODK), variable-level description (DDI-CDI), aggregate dissemination (SDMX), and cataloguing (NADA). *What must stay consistent:* variable identity from question to published aggregate — the thing that usually breaks. *Emergent outcome:* a published statistic whose every number traces to a question as asked, in the language it was asked in. *Cluster note:* ODK and XLSForm share an origin.

**CS-11 · Water safety across the chain** — `E-01` EPANET + `E-03` ODM2 + `M3-03` PHA4GE + `E-02` SWMM. *What passes:* network hydraulic model (EPANET) locating where a contaminant would travel; observations and specimens (ODM2) confirming; wastewater genomics (PHA4GE) as early signal; stormwater model (SWMM) for the surface pathway. *What must stay consistent:* site identity and spatial reference (`E-03/R2`) across models and observations.

**CS-12 · Skills-to-work chain** — `N-04` ESCO + `N-05` HR Open + `P-01` Open Badges + `P-05` LRMI. *What passes:* skill concept URIs (ESCO) into vacancy and candidate records (HR Open), into badges asserting attainment (Open Badges), into learning resources aligned to those skills (LRMI). *What must stay consistent:* the skill concept URI, across releases (`N-04/R4`) — every downstream artefact embeds it. *Emergent outcome:* a learner can see which specific learning resource closes the gap between their badges and a real vacancy. *Caveat:* two of four sources here are SNIPPET-only.

**CS-13 · Household support pathway** — `Q-03` HSDS + `T-01` C189 particulars + `T-02` FIES + `Q-05` OpenHIE alerting. *What passes:* a household's measured food insecurity (FIES) and the services available to it (HSDS), with alerting workflows (OpenHIE) reaching the household, and — where a domestic worker is employed in it — the terms-of-employment record (C189). *What must stay consistent:* household identity without creating a surveillance record; consent for contact. *Emergent outcome:* the ISIC T shortage partly addressed by composition — measurement, entitlement and referral joined, where no single source covers the household as employer and as provisioning unit.

**CS-14 · Assurance of a claim, generically** — `G-05` UNTP + `M3-04` FHIR CapabilityStatement + `J-05` C2PA + `O-01` OSCAL. *What passes:* a claim, its criteria, its evidence, and a statement of what the asserting system actually supports; C2PA binds media evidence to its origin; OSCAL expresses the control assessment behind the assertion. *What must stay consistent:* the identity of the asserter and the scope of the claim (`M3-04/R1`). *Emergent outcome:* a general pattern — *claim + criterion + evidence + declared capability* — reusable across sustainability, health and security domains.

**CS-15 · Irreversibility register** — `O-02` OCDS immutability + `S5-05` frozen preregistration + `H-05` eBL endorsement + `B-05` tailings classification. *What passes:* nothing operational — this is a **cross-domain pattern library**: four independent domains that solved "this record must not be quietly changed" differently (new release, read-only snapshot, ordered endorsement chain, disclosed classification). *What must stay consistent:* the definition of what counts as an alteration. *Emergent outcome:* a designer facing a new irreversibility problem can choose among four field-tested mechanisms with their trade-offs stated.

**CS-16 · Conditional-obligation pattern library** — `H-01` GTFS Conditionally Required + `M-01` ODK `required` expressions + `K-04` FIX Orchestra scenario conditions + `A4-05` Arena per-provider config + `F-02` BuildingSync per-use-case elements. *What passes:* five distinct encodings of "required, but only sometimes". *What must stay consistent:* the ability to explain a verdict to a human. *Emergent outcome:* a reusable design catalogue — and the observation that every one of these five specifications independently discovered that flat requiredness is insufficient.

### 4.3 Generators and configurators (each with the generator **and** a concrete product it must produce)

**GEN-1 · Conditional-requirement validator generator.** *Generator:* takes a specification's requiredness rules expressed in a small common notation (presence conditions, cross-field predicates, scenario scoping) and emits a validator that reports, per record, the condition text, the evaluated inputs and the verdict. *Concrete product it must produce:* a GTFS validator that, for `stops.txt`, prints the Conditionally Required condition from `H-01/R5`, the fields it evaluated, and PASS/FAIL — and the same generator, fed FIX Orchestra scenario rules (`K-04/R3`), produces a venue conformance suite. *Why:* CS-16 shows five specifications solved this separately; the reasoning-report property is what each one lacks.

**GEN-2 · Two-authority conformance labeller.** *Generator:* given a pair of independently versioned standards that must be cited together, emits a badge artefact and a verification endpoint. *Concrete product:* a RESO badge stating Web API version *and* Data Dictionary version (`L-01/R5`, `R6`), verifiable against a live server; the same generator produces the BuildingSync audit-level badge (`F-02`, my AC4) and the iFixit rubric-version score stamp (`S-04/R7`).

**GEN-3 · Provenance-panel builder.** *Generator:* given a schema plus a claims/evidence model, produces a per-field provenance UI showing asserter, verifier, evidence link and staleness. *Concrete product:* the PDTF buyer panel (`L-04`, my AC3) rendering title, EPC and search fields with their verification status; the same generator fed UNTP DPP claims (`G-05/R6`) produces a product-passport evidence panel.

**GEN-4 · Single-source documentation-and-schema pipeline.** *Generator:* an ODD-style pipeline taking one model source and emitting prose documentation, machine schemas, and test fixtures that cannot diverge. *Concrete product:* regenerating the IATI documentation and schema from its SSOT (`U-01/R2`) with a CI gate that fails on divergence; the same pipeline applied to Smart Data Models produces `spec.md` plus its four translations from `model.yaml` (`E-04/R3`).

**GEN-5 · Instrument-to-statistic tracer generator.** *Generator:* given a form definition and a dissemination structure, emits a variable-lineage map plus a checker that fails when a published aggregate cannot be traced to a question. *Concrete product:* for an FAO Arena forest inventory (`A4-05`), a lineage document from each XLSForm question (`S5-04`) through DDI-CDI variable descriptions (`S5-03`) to the SDMX dataflow (`S5-02`) — and a report naming any published figure without a traceable question.

**GEN-6 · Degradation-behaviour test generator.** *Generator:* takes a specification's stated degrade-not-fail and prerequisite rules and emits chaos tests. *Concrete product:* an OpenTelemetry SDK test asserting a working Meter is returned for invalid input (`J-02/R5`); the same generator fed OpenHIE workflow prerequisites (`Q-05/R1`) emits prerequisite-gate tests, and fed EEBus peer-loss conditions emits the safe-fallback test in `D-04`'s AC3.

## 5. Stress collection (54 hypothetical variations, one per selected case)

Each is **[H] hypothetical**. Each states the changed condition and its consequences. **No inherited requirement is deleted**; where a requirement becomes unsatisfiable, that is stated as a conflict to be resolved, not removed.

1. **A-01 fiboa — adversarial provenance.** *Change:* boundaries are submitted by parties with a financial interest in the outcome. *Consequence:* R5's `determination:method` becomes a claim rather than a fact; `unknown` becomes the strategically preferred value. R1–R4, R6, R7 all stand; a new evidence-binding obligation must be added on top, and AC3 (method in enumeration) no longer implies trustworthiness.

2. **A-02 Modus — lab closes mid-season.** *Change:* the issuing laboratory ceases trading with results outstanding. *Consequence:* R1's submit/result pairing has no counterparty; R5 (result links to submit) survives only if the FMIS retained the submit document. R2's method governance is unaffected. Adds a requirement for result portability to a successor lab without re-sampling.

3. **B-05 GISTM — downstream town doubles.** *Change:* population downstream grows tenfold after classification. *Consequence:* R2's consequence classification is stale though never formally invalidated; R5's disclosure now publishes a wrong classification. R3, R4 stand. Forces an explicit re-classification trigger, which the standard as I understand it does not clearly provide.

4. **B-01 OMF — v2 goes to release mid-project.** *Change:* the beta format stabilises with incompatible changes while a project is in flight. *Consequence:* R5's stability caveat becomes an active migration obligation; R1's single-file project must now declare version unambiguously (my AC3 becomes mandatory). R2–R4 stand.

5. **C-05 ONIE — the ODM abandons the platform.** *Change:* the vendor stops maintaining a machine port still in fleet use. *Consequence:* R1's additive port model now needs a custodianship path; R2's compliance environment becomes the only evidence the port still works. ## 6. Situational variation: what actually changed across the collection

The 54 cases were compared by purpose, product form, relationship structure, constraint type and operating situation. Four findings that shaped the proposals:

- **Conditional obligation is a convergent discovery.** GTFS ("Conditionally Required"), ODK (`required` as an expression), FIX Orchestra (scenario-scoped rules), BuildingSync (per-use-case required elements) and Arena (per-provider configuration) each independently concluded that flat requiredness fails. None of them can explain a verdict to a human — hence GEN-1.
- **Irreversibility is handled five different ways** (OCDS immutable releases, OSF frozen snapshots, DCSA endorsement chains, OCPI `REMOVED` status, GISTM disclosed classification). Only OCDS states a rule for the case where the irreversible record is *wrong* — and it conflicts with erasure duties (stress 29).
- **Machine-checkable conformance is routinely mistaken for fitness.** BCF viewpoints (stress 12), OMOP `gender_concept_id` (stress 34) and FHIR dual conformance (stress 48) are three domains where every stated requirement passes and the product fails. Several proposals target exactly this gap.
- **Consent and privacy appear where least expected.** OpenActive (activity leaders' qualifications), PHA4GE (sewershed re-identification), OCPI (private charging locations) and HR Open (candidate erasure) all place a consent constraint on data that looks impersonal. This is the situational axis that most often forces a different product, not just different fields.

## 6. COMPOSITION COLLECTION — larger useful wholes

Each entry states the parts, **what passes between them**, **what must remain consistent**, and **which requirements are inherited**. Composition is recursive: several of these are components of later ones.

**C1 · Water evidence chain (E-03 + E-04 + E-01 + E-02).**
Parts: DataStream's WQX JSON schema; Open Waters submission software; EPANET; SWMM. Passes: monitoring records → validated JSON → EPA-coded WQX submission → federal record; separately, network models → simulated pressure/age/overflow. Must stay consistent: **the WQX version and EPA domain-value list** used by the schema (E-03 R2) and by the translation layer (E-04 R5); the monitoring location identity across observed and simulated worlds. Inherits: E-04 R6 activity-type validation; E-01 R5 "as is" disclaimer, which forbids treating simulated results as observed evidence. Emergent outcome: a utility can state, for one catchment, what was measured, what was modelled, and where the two disagree — which neither half supports alone.

**C2 · Repair impact ledger (S-01/S-02 + S-03 + S-04).**
Parts: ORDS-structured event records; the Restart weight/CO2e dataset; the EU repair obligations. Passes: product category key → per-item weight and CO2e → aggregated avoided-impact figure → evidence for policy. Must stay consistent: **ORDS category identifiers and the ORDS version** (S-02 R1, S-03 R1) — a category rename silently breaks every historical total. Inherits: S-03 R6 (much source data lacks CO2e/weight) and R3 (per-category quality evaluation), so every aggregate must carry a quality qualifier; CC BY-SA 4.0 from both datasets, which propagates to any derived ledger. Emergent outcome: repair groups can answer a regulator's question about real-world repairability with data, and the same categories feed S-04's score debate.

**C3 · Conformance-driven standards development (H-06 + H-04 + G-01/G-02).**
Parts: a conformance framework; reference implementations; a normative schema and a conformant toolkit. Passes: scenario steps → messages → per-step results → defect reports that split "implementation wrong" from "standard ambiguous". Must stay consistent: **the standard version under test** across scenarios, reference implementation and adopter. Inherits: H-04 R3 — the reference implementation has no authorisation, so conformance testing must never run against production data. Emergent outcome: a standards body learns of its own ambiguities before adopters ship, which is the reflexive value H-06 R2 already claims and G-01/G-02's conformance claim currently lacks.

**C4 · Machine-readable regulation loop (H-01 + J-06 + N-02/N-04).**
Parts: the MDS Policy API; the UK open-standards selection process; OCDS core and its extensions. Passes: a user problem → a selected standard → published machine-readable policy → operator compliance → procurement records of the contracts that implement it. Must stay consistent: the **problem statement** from J-06 R1 through to the adopted standard; geography and organisation identifiers shared between policy and contract. Inherits: H-01 R6's 20-minute publication lead time (a hard timing contract), and N-04's additivity constraint for jurisdiction-specific fields. Emergent outcome: a city can trace a rule from the citizen problem that motivated it to the contract that delivers it.

**C5 · Building information spine (F-03 + F-04 + L-03 + N-01).**
Parts: bSDD verified dictionaries; an IDS ruleset for BIM-BEM; BuildingSync audit records; Brick operational metadata. Passes: verified concept definitions → model-checking rules → audit findings and measures → live operational points. Must stay consistent: **the term identity for a piece of equipment across design, audit and operation** — bSDD's class, the IFC entity, the BEDES term BuildingSync links to, and the Brick class. Inherits: F-03 R13 (unit must match dimension) and R14 (do not recreate IFC properties); L-03 R7 (examples must stay valid); N-01 R2 (relationship minimality). Emergent outcome: a measure proposed in an audit can be checked against the design model and verified against operational data — the three today live in unconnected tools.

**C6 · Portable evidence for health decisions (Q-04 + Q-05 + Q-07).**
Parts: US Core profiles; CARIN Blue Button consumer claims; OMOP CDM and its analysis tools. Passes: clinical records → profiled FHIR resources → consumer-visible claims; separately, site data → OMOP → portable cohort definitions → summary results. Must stay consistent: **patient identity within a site, and concept identity across sites** (Q-07 R2 vocabulary version). Inherits: Q-04 R3 Must-Support obligations; Q-05's caregiver delegation scope; Q-07's constraint that analyses travel and data does not. Emergent outcome: a claim a patient can read and a cohort a researcher can run are derived from the same underlying record without the record leaving its custodian.

**C7 · Identity continuity across a shutdown (U-04 + O-04 + O-06 + J-04).**
Parts: a decommissioning humanitarian identity provider; a national identity provider; a wallet presentation protocol; an agent-protocol authorization design. Passes: subject identifiers and account mappings from the retiring provider to a successor; presentations for credentials the successor cannot itself assert. Must stay consistent: **the subject identifier per relying party** — the thing that actually breaks at U-04 R4's 31 December 2025 cut-off. Inherits: J-04 R2 (clients must not hold third-party credentials); O-06 R2 (three credential formats). Emergent outcome: a migration plan in which relying platforms keep working, which no single source provides.

**C8 · Agricultural decision provenance (A-01 + A-05 + A-06 + A-03's discipline).**
Parts: BrAPI trial and germplasm data; ADAPT production data; Modus lab results. Passes: work order → sample → analyte result → agronomic recommendation → field operation record → observation. Must stay consistent: **sample identity from order to result** (A-06 R5), **germplasm and study identity** (A-01 R2/R5), and **unit abbreviations** (A-05 R1) across all three. Inherits: A-06's requirement that method identity accompany every analyte; A-05's data-only constraint, so the composition must not smuggle in a toolkit dependency. Emergent outcome: a recommendation can be traced back to the specific sample, method and trial that justify it.

**C9 · Preservation-grade cultural publishing (R-02 + R-05 + R-06 + X-03).**
Parts: OCFL storage layout; a museum IIIF service; a manifest editor; TEI-encoded texts. Passes: source images and transcriptions → OCFL object versions → IIIF manifests → viewers. Must stay consistent: **the object identifier across storage, manifest and citation**, and the rights status attached to it (R-05 R6). Inherits: R-02's self-describing-storage requirement; R-06's obligation that exports open in independent viewers; X-03's prose-schema parity. Emergent outcome: a citation that still resolves after the publishing software is gone — the actual preservation promise.

**C10 · Grid change with a safe floor (D-02 + D-05).**
Parts: PACS configuration management; household export control. Passes: protection settings and network limits downward; DER capability and status upward. Must stay consistent: **the equipment identity and the active limit** between substation configuration and edge control. Inherits: D-05 R5's default fallback control and D-02 R2's edition gate. Emergent outcome: a change at the substation and a change at the inverter can be reasoned about as one system, with a defined safe state when the link fails.

**C11 · Generator — Conformance-Pack Builder (configurator).**
Generator: given (i) a normative schema or API description, (ii) a party-role model, and (iii) a set of acceptance criteria, it emits a runnable conformance pack — scenarios, synthetic fixtures, a counterparty simulator, and a report template that separates implementation defects from standard ambiguities. Inputs it needs: H-06's scenario/party model; G-01's schema; A-01's module structure as a second worked case. **Concrete product it must produce:** a conformance pack for **BrAPI v2.1 Phenotyping**, containing scenarios covering Observation Unit, Observation, Observation Variable, Trait, Scale and Method retrieval; fixtures where trait/scale/method are deliberately mismatched; and a pass/fail report that names which of A-01 R3's entities failed. Invariants: the pack must cite the exact specification version; fixtures must be synthetic (inherited from U-05 R3 where sensitive domains are involved).

**C12 · Generator — Requirement-Card Compiler (configurator).**
Generator: consumes a specification's requirement list (e.g. F-03's DCT/GEN/CLS/PRP/CPR/REL IDs, or B-03's GeMS level criteria) and emits, per requirement, a card carrying the requirement ID, its verbatim text, whether it is machine-checkable, the check if so, and the acceptance evidence if not. **Concrete product it must produce:** a 20-card deck for **bSDD verification**, with GEN-01, PRP-01, PRP-03, PRP-04, CLS-02, CLS-05 and REL-01 marked machine-checkable and carrying executable checks, and the ISO 704 definitional requirements (GEN-09/10/11) marked human-judged with a reviewer prompt. Inherits F-03's finding that **no numeric thresholds exist**, so the compiler must not invent them — a card without a threshold must say so.

- **P-071 · Protocol Item Concordance (document component).** For CB-M04. A required front-matter table mapping each of the 33 SPIRIT items to its protocol location, updated with every amendment and showing which items an amendment touched. Value: reviewers reconstruct this by hand; amendments are where outcome-switching hides.
- **P-072 · Convention Disclosure Block (metadata convention).** For CB-Q02. A short machine-readable block published alongside every federated study result stating vocabulary version and site ETL conventions. Value: `Q02-R5` says conventions are not version-tracked; this makes the untracked thing explicit at the point of use.
- **P-073 · Version-Boundary Annotation (analysis practice).** For CB-L03/CB-P04. Any longitudinal count crossing a standards effective date carries a visible boundary marker and a statement of which elements changed. Value: annual standards revisions create artificial trend breaks that get reported as findings.

### 3.9 Cluster: cross-domain transplants (deliberately unfamiliar combinations)

- **P-080 · HACCP for sanitation, in reverse: CCP thinking for data pipelines.** From CB-E03/CB-I01 into CB-Q02/CB-U05. Treat a humanitarian or federated-research data pipeline as a service chain with hazard events (mis-merge, identity collision, silent truncation), critical control points, operational limits and corrective actions defined *before* breakthrough. Value: data quality is usually inspected at the end; sanitation safety planning is an established discipline for controlling a chain you cannot re-run.
- **P-081 · Consequence classification for software systems.** From CB-B02 into CB-J05/CB-Q04. Classify integrations by potential consequence (Low → Extreme) using downstream-harm criteria rather than technical criticality, and set review depth and drill frequency from the class. Value: tailings dam practice ranks by what happens downstream, not by asset value — an inversion most software risk registers do not make.
- **P-082 · Covert inspection applied to safeguarding.** From CB-U04 into CB-Q03/CB-L03. A mechanism letting an authorised party verify a service's protected/registered status without the service learning it was checked. Value: in homelessness and human-services contexts, the act of checking can itself expose someone.
- **P-083 · Proxy-time scoring applied to bureaucratic burden.** From CB-S03 into CB-T03/CB-O04. Score a public process from 0–10 using preassigned proxy times for each required action and a scaling factor for each required tool or document, weighted by how unavoidable the step is. Value: administrative burden is discussed anecdotally; iFixit's method is a proven way to make a subjective difficulty comparable across designs.
- **P-084 · Golden thread applied to a research protocol.** From CB-F03 into CB-M04. A single accountable, digital, handover-obligated record of everything affecting the safety of trial participants, transferred with criminal-liability-grade formality when the sponsor or PI changes. Value: building safety solved the custody-of-safety-information problem after a disaster; trials transfer custody routinely with far less ceremony.
- **P-085 · Event-centric documentation applied to machine maintenance.** From CB-R07 into CB-C01/CB-B01. Model a machine's life as CRM-style events (installation, modification, failure, repair) rather than as a current-state record. Value: MTConnect gives the present; museums learned long ago that provenance is a chain of events, and maintenance history is provenance.
- **P-086 · Trait/method/scale applied to social indicators.** From CB-A05 into CB-N03/CB-P04. Require that every reported social or education indicator be a declared triple of concept + measurement method + scale, with mandatory identifier reuse. Value: crop science solved "plant height measured three ways" with a structural rule; education and labour statistics still argue about it in prose.
- **G-analogy note.** These transplants are the ones I judged non-trivial: each moves a *mechanism*, not a vocabulary.

## 4. COMPOSITION — larger useful wholes

Each system names its parts, what passes between them, what must stay consistent, and which requirements it inherits. Recursive composition is used: several systems take an earlier system as a component.

**S-1 · Repair Evidence System** — parts: repair-event log (CB-S01 ORDS), impact method (CB-S01), repairability scores (CB-S03, CB-S02), P-052 generator, P-090 wall chart.
*What passes:* device category, fault, outcome, footprint value + source year, quality rating, score generation.
*Must stay consistent:* ORDS category definitions between log and footprint table; rubric generation between any two compared scores.
*Emergent outcome:* a repair group can answer a council's "what does this achieve?" and a resident's "which one should I buy?" from one dataset.
*Inherited requirements:* `S01-R1`–`R7` in full, including the approximation caveat (`S01-R7`) which must appear on generator output; `S03-R6`/`R8` category-specific weights and annual revision.

**S-2 · Field Identity Fabric** — parts: AgStack registry (CB-A02), P-001 Tolerance Atlas, P-003 Same-Thing Ledger, GDST lot events (CB-A04), STDM/LADM tenure records (CB-L02/L05), P-094 Boundary Walk.

*Inherited requirements:* `A02-R2` (only the GeoID returns — so the fabric must not leak identity), `A04-R3` EPCIS basis, `L05-R3` common structure.

**S-3 · Chain-of-Custody Stack for seafood** — recursive: takes S-2 as a component. Parts: GDST CTEs (CB-A04), GS1 EPCIS/CBV (CB-G02), Peppol invoice (CB-G01), P-032 second-reader logic adapted to aggregation events.

**S-4 · Grid-Edge Settlement Loop** — parts: OCPI (CB-D01), OpenADR programs/events (CB-D04), CSIP/IEEE 2030.5 device control (CB-D03), Green Button consented usage (CB-D05), CalTRACK savings method (CB-D02), P-020 revocation harness.

## 5. SITUATIONAL VARIATION — how each variation changes the product

- **V-1 Interruption (CB-P05, CB-Q01).** When schooling or care is interrupted rather than continuous, the product must be *resumable from partial state*: cohort definitions become person-level continuity records, and re-registration from zero becomes a defect rather than an inconvenience.
- **V-2 Divided authority (CB-G03, CB-B05, CB-O03, CB-L03).** Where approval sits with one body and delivery with another, revocation becomes the design centre: the product needs a propagation path and a measurable latency (P-020), not just a grant path.
- **V-3 Irreversible effects (CB-K02, CB-C05, CB-E05).** Error handling must be compensating rather than corrective, which changes the interface: there is no edit affordance, only a new authorised counter-action with a traceable link.
- **V-4 Concurrency (CB-F04, CB-D04, CB-K02).** Several parties act on one object at once: the product must attribute duty and effect per actor per moment — the fall-protection duty attaches to *each employee on a surface*, not to the surface.
- **V-5 Scarce resources (CB-M08, CB-T05, CB-E02).** Compute, test capacity or field staff are limited: the product's job shifts from "capture everything" to "decide what not to capture, visibly" — which is precisely what a Data Request is.
- **V-6 Changing evidence (CB-L03, CB-P04, CB-S03, CB-M05).** Definitions revise on a schedule: every output needs a version stamp and every cross-boundary comparison a visible warning; trend analysis acquires a mandatory boundary annotation.
- **V-7 Competing needs (CB-O03, CB-U04, CB-B05).** Privacy against accountability, protection against targetability: the product cannot optimise one; it must expose the trade-off as a configurable, reviewable decision with a named decision-maker.
- **V-8 Uncertainty in the number itself (CB-S01, CB-S03).** Where the headline figure rests on an assumption the source itself calls approximate, the caveat becomes a rendering requirement, not documentation.
- **V-9 The beneficiary has no organisation (CB-T01–T05).** Products must be single-artefact, physical or one-page, and must not assume a system of record. This is why §3.7 favours cards, charts and one-off documents over software.
- **V-10 The producer has no capability (CB-R04, CB-U05, CB-I02).** Adoption mechanics dominate: a standard applied by hand in a spreadsheet mid-crisis (`U05-R2` adds a row rather than replacing headers) beats a better model requiring tooling.
- **V-11 Discovery vs. expression (CB-R01, CB-R08 vs. CB-R05, CB-R06).** Discovery products optimise for aggregation and minimum mandatory metadata; expression products optimise for encoding nuance. Combining them in S-11 requires that the discovery layer never becomes the ceiling for the expression layer.
- **V-12 Care vs. coordination (CB-Q01 vs. CB-P05).** A data element that changes a clinical decision has a different error cost from one that feeds a monthly indicator; P-092 makes that distinction visible at the point of entry, which no shared data dictionary does by itself.
- **V-13 Maintenance as the primary situation (CB-C01, CB-F02, CB-S03).** When the product's purpose is keeping something working, event history outranks current state (P-085), and repairability outranks initial quality.
- **V-14 Creation under constraint (CB-C03, CB-R02).** Where the maker is also the maintainer (GVCS), modularity and documentation for multiple audiences are functional requirements, not niceties: `C03-R2` and `C03-R3` exist because the same person builds, fixes and teaches.

- **Randomness tool:** Linux kernel CSPRNG via `getrandom(2)`, accessed as `python3 -c "import secrets; secrets.randbits(64)"` on `Linux-6.18.44-fc-v24-x86_64-with-glibc2.39`. ## 7. STRESS COLLECTION — one hypothetical variation per selected case

Every entry is **hypothetical**. Each states the changed condition and its consequences; inherited requirements remain in force unless the entry says a requirement is *contradicted*, in which case the conflict is named rather than resolved silently.

1. **CB-A03-X.** *Changed:* a herd runs three AMS brands and one brand's ID reader degrades to 94%. *Consequence:* `A03-R3` fails at device level while `A03-R1`, `R2`, `R4`, `R5` remain in force; the herd's records are still linkable but the certification claim is not. The product must be able to publish *partial* certification with a per-device read-rate disclosure — which the current pass/fail gate cannot express.
2. **CB-A02-X.** *Changed:* two co-ops register overlapping polygons for the same field after a boundary dispute. *Consequence:* `A02-R3` determinism does not adjudicate; the registry must hold two GeoIDs and a recorded overlap, and downstream products (S-2) must refuse to silently pick one. P-094 becomes a required, not optional, component.
3. **CB-B01-X.** *Changed:* a block model exceeds container size limits and must be delivered in parts. *Consequence:* `B01-R1`/`R2` element support is unchanged, but round-trip attribute binding (`B01-AC2`) now spans files; a part-manifest and a completeness assertion become necessary, and the "single interchange file" assumption of `B01-R5` is contradicted.
4. **CB-B05-X.** *Changed:* a named beneficial owner faces credible threats after publication. *Consequence:* `B05-R2` (PEP disclosure regardless of level) and the public-knowledge objective `B05-R1` collide with duty of care. P-022's safety review becomes mandatory; the unresolved question — whether any redaction is permissible under the Standard — is retained, not answered.
5. **CB-C03-X.** *Changed:* a module is redesigned for a different hydraulic standard in one region. *Consequence:* `C03-R2` parallel development survives only if interface compatibility is versioned; `C03-R4`'s 8× cost target may be met regionally but not globally, so cost claims must become region-scoped.
6. **CB-C05-X.** *Changed:* a device's owner ceases to exist (company dissolved) with ownership never transferred. *Consequence:* `C05-R1` ownership transfer has no counterparty; the device is functional but permanently unupdatable. Trademark conformance (`C05-R3`) is unaffected, which reveals that conformance says nothing about end-of-life custody.
7. **CB-D05-X.** *Changed:* the regulator withdraws a third party's qualification while active grants exist. *Consequence:* `D05-R2` is retrospectively violated by ongoing delivery; `D05-R1`'s custodian duty now requires proactive termination. The reference stack's module separation (`D05-R4`) helps only if the authorization server subscribes to qualification status — which is not stated.
8. **CB-D01-X.** *Changed:* a tariff changes while a session is in progress. *Consequence:* `D01-R3`'s Tariffs and CDRs modules remain required, but `D01-AC2` (CDR recomputes from the tariff referenced at session start) becomes ambiguous. The unresolved question from the brief is now load-bearing and must be answered by scheme rules outside OCPI.
9. **CB-E05-X.** *Changed:* a consignment is 60% functional units, 40% untested. *Consequence:* `E05-R2`/`R3` give no mixed category; `E05-R5` PIC applies to the waste fraction. Either the consignment is split before movement or it is classified wholly as waste — the guidelines' silence on mixed lots is the gap, and P-032's second-reader applies to the non-waste fraction only.
10. **CB-E03-X.** *Changed:* informal emptiers, who are outside the utility's authority, are the dominant conveyance step. *Consequence:* `E03-R1` requires the whole chain in scope, but `E03-R3`/`R5` monitoring and correction presume managerial authority. The plan must substitute incentive and access mechanisms for control, and the acceptance criterion `E03-AC3` becomes unachievable for that step unless the exposure group is protected by other means.
11. **CB-F02-X.** *Changed:* an application needs a point type Brick does not define. *Consequence:* `F02-R3`'s minimal relationship set and `F02-R6` backwards compatibility hold, but portability (`F02-AC2`) breaks for that application. A local extension must be declared as an extension, or the model silently becomes non-portable while still validating.
12. **CB-F04-X.** *Changed:* three employers' crews occupy one surface simultaneously. *Consequence:* `F04-R1` duty attaches per employee per employer; `F04-R3` determination is a single physical fact but a triplicated legal duty. P-070's posted slip must name all three employers' determinations, or one employer's compliance conceals another's gap.
13. **CB-G03-X.** *Changed:* an accredited entity's accreditation lapses. *Consequence:* every employee authorisation it issued is of uncertain status; `G03-R6`'s two-layer requirement gives no cascade rule. Independent operators lose security-feature access through no act of their own, and `G03-AC1`'s unrestricted non-security access must be shown to survive the lapse.
