## 11. Executed bounded coverage checker: CK01

Edition date: 2026-09-10. Implementation: framework-coverage-checker 0.1.0. State: EXECUTED_BOUNDED_REGRESSION; FULL_FRAMEWORK_QUALIFICATION_OPEN. The implementation and retained run evidence accompany this specification. No P1–P349 record is promoted to a full execution or integration pass by this amendment.

### CK01.1 Experiment boundary

World-case generation is independent of the framework. It produces practical situations, activities and outcomes across industries, circumstances and scales. It receives no family catalogue, framework grammar, proof records, family-derived composition templates or mapping feedback. Independently gathered real-world needs can ground situations; they do not replace the practical case as the generated object. Freeze the original case and its source identity before reverse engineering. Do not silently repair the case during mapping, discard an inconvenient requirement or use a proposed framework extension as baseline evidence.

The reverse-engineering side may use every relevant full family and proof record. A missing entry in a short family-anchor list is a retrieval issue until the full catalogue is inspected. A proof record without case-bound qualification remains a proof-layer gap. Preserve distinct findings for a missing case fact, incoherent case connection, absent accepted encoding, absent implementation, missing adapter, missing proof, failed candidate and incomplete search. Only a supported absence claim for a declared complete search space establishes absence within that space.

The objective remains finding precise hardening items. Counts of family names or optimistic labels are not the success criterion. Coverage requires every original requirement and its inputs, relationships, constraints, acceptance and evidence to be reconstructed.

The complete dependency lock accompanies the implementation. These are isolated CK01 pins. They do not replace or qualify L07, L08 or L09's historical version profiles.

The construction predicate language admits quantifier-free Boolean and linear integer/rational formulas. Rationals use exact fraction strings. Floats, nonlinear multiplication, arbitrary division, quantifiers and executable case text are rejected. Symbolic case inputs are valid when their meaning and admitted domain are explicit. A demand for concrete fixture values is not substituted for that contract.

The static construction kernel admits Seq, Join and ForkJoin DAGs only, with all selected ports explicitly bound. Other grammar forms receive UNSUPPORTED_ENCODING. The separate finite-state and obligation commands check their supplied models; their results do not automatically qualify a static construction's temporal or interaction semantics. Shared resources and effects need accepted global requirements.

### CK01.3 Result semantics

CASE_FORMALIZATION_REQUIRED means the source summary lacks an accepted formal reconstruction input. MODEL_RECONSTRUCTED means one finite candidate satisfies the encoded contracts conditional on its component axioms. PROOF_GAP means that candidate still lacks the selected implementation evidence. NO_CONSTRUCTION_IN_DECLARED_CATALOGUE_AND_BOUNDS is limited to the supplied catalogue, identity-compatible edges and explicit component bound. SEARCH_INCOMPLETE preserves solver unknown, disagreement and search/candidate limits. UNSUPPORTED_ENCODING identifies a capability the checker does not encode.

FINITE_SAFETY_PASS and FINITE_RULE_PARITY_PASS apply only to the exact supplied finite models. REPLAY_MATCH means new solver execution matched the retained decisions and query identities. Neither status establishes real-world case coverage.

CK01 version 0.1.0 has no unconditional COVERED status and no metadata switch that bypasses missing execution evidence. It can establish conditional model reconstruction, concrete encoded counterexamples and evidence gaps. Full coverage requires a qualified implementation registry, actual adapter/consumer paths, accepted case semantics and the inherited end-to-end gates. An ACCEPTED metadata value records a review assertion; it does not prove the review was faithful.

The corpus run processed all 5,000 cases and preserved 131,659 source-clause occurrences and 19,894 activity/product occurrences. It indexed 38 families and 349 proof records. It retained the prior 52 individual summary reviews as review aids, without claiming that those reviews are construction proofs or that all 5,000 cases were manually reconstructed.

All 5,000 cases receive CASE_FORMALIZATION_REQUIRED and NO_CASE_BOUND_EXECUTION_WITNESS. Full coverage certificates: zero. Proved uncovered cases: zero. These counts do not imply zero conceptual coverage or poor family coverage. They identify the limits of the supplied summaries and qualification evidence.

The structural findings include 31 cases with an input placeholder, 14 strict-covered/lenient-partial records needing verdict-semantics or trace review, and 3,321 cases with at least one repeated input, part or deliverable label whose multiplicity is unresolved. Repetition is retained. It is not automatically treated as duplication to delete. All root product labels appear among their case's part outputs, and all parsed literal deadlines are within their literal windows; neither check establishes causal reconstruction or scheduling feasibility.

The final development run collected and passed 45 tests with zero failures, errors or skips. Three generated-input tests each executed 100 passing examples with zero failing or invalid examples. One test body was generated unchanged by hypothesis.extra.ghostwriter.equivalent using unittest style and TestCase.assertEqual. Its two diagnostic targets call separate Z3 and cvc5 encoders. The other test bodies are authored development tests, not qualifying inherited GP01 guards. The project-owned diagnostic bindings remain disclosed; passing this suite does not close P344's full public-callable/adaptor qualification contract.

Executed demonstrations include a three-stage backward construction, shared-capacity failure with a counterexample, incompatible evidence basis, finite post-revocation effect reachability and a multi-feature resource obligation. Demonstrations are separately authored models; they are never counted as completed original cases. Baseline and amended framework editions, case dossiers, solver traces, tests and source hashes are retained in the accompanying package.
