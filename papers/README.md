# Papers

This folder holds the research papers behind the Compiled AI method, the bibliography they rest on, and the record of what has been checked.

## Rule for this folder

Nothing goes in here without a verifiable source. A paper of our own carries its date and SHA-256. A third-party reference carries the URL that was fetched to confirm its title and authors, and the date it was fetched. A claim that could not be checked is marked as such rather than dropped or guessed.
