## 0. METHOD, CLASSIFICATIONS, SCOPE

**Classifications used**
- **UN ISIC Rev. 5** (endorsed by the UN Statistical Commission at its 54th session, March 2023; 21 sections A–U at the top level). Verified via [UNSD ISIC](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/isic) and [Introduction to ISIC Rev.5](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/ISIC5_Intro_11Mar2024.pdf) — SNIPPET. Section letters A–U are stable between Rev.4 and Rev.5, so cases assigned by letter are valid under either; where a case's activity moved between revisions I note it.
- **OECD Fields of Research and Development (FORD)**, Frascati Manual 2015 revised FOS classification: 6 major fields / 42 minor. Verified via [Frascati Manual 2015](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/frascati-manual-2015_9789264239012-en.html) and [ARIS FORD listing](https://www.aris-rs.si/en/gradivo/sifranti/inc/FORD1-eng.pdf) — SNIPPET. Fields: F1 Natural sciences, F2 Engineering and technology, F3 Medical and health sciences, F4 Agricultural and veterinary sciences, F5 Social sciences, F6 Humanities and the arts.

**Coverage achieved:** 27 categories (21 ISIC + 6 FORD). Every category has ≥5 eligible cases from distinct originating projects/organisations. Per-category counts: A5 B5 C5 D5 E5 F5 G5 H5 I6 J5 K5 L5 M9 N5 O5 P5 Q5 R8 S5 T6 U6 | F1:6 F2:15 F3:7 F4:5 F5:13 F6:6. **No category shortage.** Shortages of a different kind (source-access, not case-count) are recorded in §0.3.

**Read-status convention**
- `READ` — original text fetched and read.
- `READ-P` — original repository/document fetched, but the fetched file carried only part of the requirement text (usually a README rather than the normative body); everything drawn from it is limited to what that file actually said.
- `SNIPPET` — search-result titles/snippets only. **Every requirement taken from a SNIPPET source is unverified against the original.**

25 cases are READ, 17 READ-P, 70 SNIPPET.

**0.1 Environment constraint that shaped the corpus.** WebFetch in this environment resolves only `github.com` and `raw.githubusercontent.com`; every other host returns EGRESS_BLOCKED. I therefore biased discovery towards primary sources whose normative text is version-controlled on GitHub, and accepted snippet status for regulation, ISO/OASIS/Codex/WHO/ILO texts, and PDFs hosted elsewhere. This is a *sampling bias in the corpus*, not a claim about which needs matter: standards published as PDFs by treaty bodies are under-read relative to their real-world weight.

**0.2 Origin-cluster rule.** Copied specifications, shared templates and trivial variants are grouped. Clusters recorded in the inventory. Two cluster caveats:
- **OC-MOBILITYDATA:** GTFS (CB-H01) and GBFS (CB-H02) are distinct originating projects (GTFS from Google/TriMet, 2006; GBFS from NABSA) but are **today co-stewarded by MobilityData**. The sampler treated them as distinct clusters and drew both for section H. I record this rather than silently redraw: they are distinct specifications with distinct governance histories and distinct normative content, but they are not distinct *stewards* today.
- **OC-WHO** spans three distinct WHO programmes (Sanitation Safety Planning CB-E03, SMART Guidelines CB-Q01, HWT Scheme CB-T05). Treated as distinct projects; shared parent recorded.
- **GDST (CB-A04) is built on GS1 EPCIS (CB-G02)** — distinct organisations, inherited technical substrate. Recorded as a dependency, not a cluster merge.

**0.3 Recorded scope decisions**
1. **Brief depth.** Full construction briefs (§2) are written for the 54 sampled cases. The remaining 58 sourced cases appear in the frozen inventory (§1) with origin, source, URL, version/date and read status, but not with full atomic-requirement decomposition. Reason: 112 full briefs exceeds a returnable message. This is a deliberate, recorded narrowing of *presentation*, not of discovery — the inventory is complete and was frozen before sampling.
2. **Cross-listing.** Three cases are eligible in two categories (CB-C03 in C and T; CB-G05 in G and I; CB-P05 in P and U). Sampling was without replacement across the whole draw, so a cross-listed case selected once cannot be drawn again.
3. **Interpretation of "eligible."** A case is eligible if a primary source states a required outcome or constraint that some identifiable party must satisfy. Marketing pages and secondary commentary do not qualify on their own; two cases (CB-C04 EU DPP, CB-G04 ARTS/UnifiedPOS) rest partly on secondary commentary and are flagged `SNIPPET-SECONDARY`.

### A — Agriculture, forestry and fishing

**CB-A03 · ICAR animal data exchange + cattle milk recording · ISIC A · FORD 4**
*Beneficiary/situation:* A dairy herd manager and the national milk-recording organisation that certifies her yields; robotic milking systems from three vendors write to three formats, and payment, breeding value and certification all hang on the same records. *Source:* ICAR, "Section 2 — Guidelines for Dairy Cattle Milk Recording" and "Section 15 — Guidelines for Data Exchange", https://www.icar.org/Guidelines/02-Overview-Cattle-Milk-Recording.pdf and https://www.icar.org/Guidelines/15-Data-Exchange.pdf, plus ADE standards page — **SNIPPET** (all requirements below unverified against the original).
*Required deliverables (source):* a data-exchange specification usable between recording devices and recording databases; approval/checking procedures for milk-recording devices.
*Available inputs:* AMS/parlour event streams, ICAR-approved animal ID devices, lab sample results.
*Atomic requirements:* `A03-R1` [S] the animal must be marked with an ICAR-approved identification device or system. `A03-R2` [S] the sample, the milk weight and the cow ID must be linked at the milking. `A03-R3` [S] milk-recording devices must read animal identification with ≥98% success rate. `A03-R4` [S] AMS data may be exchanged either in the manufacturer's format on request or via the ICAR ADE milking-data standard. `A03-R5` [S] the ADE specification is open-source and free to use and covers dairy cows, milking sheep, beef cattle, sheep, farmed buffalo and deer.
*Component relationships:* ID device → milking event → sample vial → lab result. What passes: `animalID`, `milkingTimestamp`, `milkWeight`, `sampleID`. Must remain consistent: the animal identity across all four, and the milking occasion as the join key (`A03-R2` is precisely the anti-decoupling requirement).
*Whole-product constraints:* species coverage beyond cattle; vendor neutrality; the 98% read rate is a device-level gate that constrains system-level claims.
*Acceptance criteria:* `A03-AC1` **(source)** device ID read success ≥98% under the approval/checking procedure. `A03-AC2` **(source)** every recorded milk weight resolves to exactly one cow ID and one sample. `A03-AC3` **(proposed — requires acceptance)** a round-trip test moves one herd-day of AMS data through ADE and back with zero loss of animal-occasion linkage.
*Assumptions/unknowns:* the exact ADE message schema, its versioning policy, and whether the 98% figure is per-device or per-installation were not verified. Unresolved: whether "success rate" is measured on first read or after retries — this materially changes device design.

**CB-A02 · AgStack Asset Registry (GeoID) · ISIC A · FORD 4**
*Beneficiary/situation:* A smallholder co-op agronomist and the buyer's due-diligence team need to say "this is the same field" across a carbon registry, a traceability system and a satellite analytics vendor, without any of them sharing farmer identity. *Source:* AgStack/Linux Foundation, asset-registry, https://github.com/agstack/asset-registry — **READ-P** (README only; endpoint list and matching rules not in the fetched file). Supporting: Linux Foundation press announcement of a global field-boundary dataset — SNIPPET.
*Required deliverables (source):* an API that registers a geospatial asset and returns a unique identifier.
*Available inputs:* field polygons as WKT, GPS traces, mobile capture (e.g. TerraTrac).
*Atomic requirements:* `A02-R1` [S] register an asset — typically a polygon, but any geospatial geometry — and return a 256-bit / 16-character alphanumeric unique ID (GeoID). `A02-R2` [S] the API registers the asset using its WKT representation and returns only the GeoID. `A02-R3` [S] GeoIDs are deterministic identifiers for any geospatial feature (fields, points, forests, countries). `A02-R4` [I] because only the GeoID is returned, the registry is usable as a privacy-preserving join key: ownership data need not travel with it.

*Acceptance criteria:* `A02-AC1` **(source)** registering a WKT polygon returns a 16-character alphanumeric GeoID. `A02-AC2` **(proposed — requires acceptance)** two independent field walks of the same parcel, differing by ≤5 m RMS, resolve to a matched GeoID under the registry's matching call.
*Assumptions/unknowns:* the S2-cell approach and the tolerance/matching semantics were not in the fetched README — genuinely open. Feasibility question retained: is deterministic identity under geometric noise achieved by tolerance matching, by canonicalisation, or not at all?

*Beneficiary/situation:* A small regional archive that wants its digitised photographs findable in a European portal, and an aggregator that must accept data from hundreds of such providers without editing each by hand. *Source:* Europeana Foundation, EDM Mapping Guidelines v2.3 (Nov 2016) and EDM Definition v5.2.8 (Oct 2017), https://pro.europeana.eu/files/Europeana_Professional/Share_your_data/Technical_requirements/EDM_Documentation/EDM_Mapping_Guidelines_v2.3_112016.pdf — **SNIPPET**.
*Required deliverables (source):* provider data mapped to EDM, with property obligation levels observed.
*Available inputs:* provider catalogues, the mapping guidelines with worked examples and diagrams.
*Atomic requirements:* `R08-R1` [S] EDM is a theoretical data model allowing data to be presented in different ways according to the practices of the various domains contributing to Europeana. `R08-R2` [S] the Mapping Guidelines define properties, the data types usable as values, and the obligation level of each property. `R08-R3` [S] `edm:isShownAt` or `edm:isShownBy` are mandatory properties. `R08-R4` [S] the Provided Cultural Heritage Object (`edm:ProvidedCHO`) denotes the original object — physical (painting, book) or born-digital (3D model) — which is the focus of description and search. `R08-R5` [S] Aggregations aggregate resources; `ore:aggregates` expresses that the object resource is a member of the set of aggregated resources of the subject Aggregation. `R08-R6` [S] documentation includes original data, its EDM conversion, and diagrams of property distribution across classes, alongside content and metadata tier examples.
*Component relationships:* provider record → ProvidedCHO + Aggregation + WebResource → portal. What passes: the CHO description and the links to the digital representation. Must stay consistent: the separation between the object and its digital surrogate — collapsing them is the classic aggregation error.
*Whole-product constraints:* providers have unequal capability and the model must degrade gracefully across metadata tiers; links to provider sites break over time, and `edm:isShownAt` is exactly such a link.
*Acceptance criteria:* `R08-AC1` **(source)** every record carries `edm:isShownAt` or `edm:isShownBy`. `R08-AC2` **(source)** the ProvidedCHO is distinguished from its aggregation and web resources. `R08-AC3` **(proposed — requires acceptance)** link rot in `edm:isShownAt` is detected and reported to the provider rather than silently degrading the portal.
*Assumptions/unknowns:* guideline versions are from 2016/2017; a newer EDM revision may exist and was not located.

## 3. PROPOSAL COLLECTION — invented constructions (separately labelled)

Everything in §3 is **invented by me**. None of it is required by any source. Each entry names the case it serves, the beneficiary, the product form, and why it is materially different from its siblings — not merely a rename. Grouped so that each cluster shows *several materially different constructions for one need*.

### 3.1 Cluster: identity of a thing across parties (CB-A02, CB-M05, CB-A05, CB-L05)

- **P-001 · Tolerance Atlas (research instrument).** For CB-A02. A published empirical study and open dataset measuring how far two independent field-boundary tracings of the same parcel diverge, by capture method (walked GPS, satellite delineation, cadastral import) and by terrain. Beneficiary: registry designers. Value: the GeoID matching tolerance is currently a design guess; this converts it into a measured parameter. Different in kind from the registry itself — it is evidence, not software.
- **P-002 · Boundary Reconciliation Clinic (service + operating procedure).** For CB-A02. A field procedure a co-op runs quarterly: farmers bring conflicting boundary claims, a facilitator walks disputed edges with two devices, and the session ends with either a merged GeoID or a *recorded disagreement* with both geometries retained. Value: the registry treats disagreement as an error; the clinic treats it as a state to be documented, which is what land conflicts actually require.
- **P-003 · "Same-Thing" Ledger (coordinated system).** Across CB-A02, CB-M05, CB-A05. A small, boring service whose only job is to record *assertions of sameness* between identifiers from different schemes ("this GeoID = this cadastral parcel = this traceability lot"), each assertion signed, dated and revocable. Value: every one of these standards mints identifiers and none of them owns the cross-walk; today the cross-walk lives in spreadsheets. Materially different from a registry: it never mints identity, only claims about identity.
- **P-004 · Algorithm-Version Tripwire (maintenance tool).** For CB-M05. - **P-005 · Variable Minting Referee (generator + governance procedure).** For CB-A05. ## 4. COMPOSITION — larger useful wholes

Each system names its parts, what passes between them, what must stay consistent, and which requirements it inherits. Recursive composition is used: several systems take an earlier system as a component.

### Generators and configurators (each with the concrete product it must produce)

## 5. SITUATIONAL VARIATION — how each variation changes the product

- **V-1 Interruption (CB-P05, CB-Q01).** When schooling or care is interrupted rather than continuous, the product must be *resumable from partial state*: cohort definitions become person-level continuity records, and re-registration from zero becomes a defect rather than an inconvenience.
- **V-2 Divided authority (CB-G03, CB-B05, CB-O03, CB-L03).** Where approval sits with one body and delivery with another, revocation becomes the design centre: the product needs a propagation path and a measurable latency (P-020), not just a grant path.
- **V-3 Irreversible effects (CB-K02, CB-C05, CB-E05).** Error handling must be compensating rather than corrective, which changes the interface: there is no edit affordance, only a new authorised counter-action with a traceable link.
- **V-4 Concurrency (CB-F04, CB-D04, CB-K02).** Several parties act on one object at once: the product must attribute duty and effect per actor per moment — the fall-protection duty attaches to *each employee on a surface*, not to the surface.
- **V-5 Scarce resources (CB-M08, CB-T05, CB-E02).** Compute, test capacity or field staff are limited: the product's job shifts from "capture everything" to "decide what not to capture, visibly" — which is precisely what a Data Request is.
- **V-6 Changing evidence (CB-L03, CB-P04, CB-S03, CB-M05).** Definitions revise on a schedule: every output needs a version stamp and every cross-boundary comparison a visible warning; trend analysis acquires a mandatory boundary annotation.
- **V-7 Competing needs (CB-O03, CB-U04, CB-B05).** Privacy against accountability, protection against targetability: the product cannot optimise one; it must expose the trade-off as a configurable, reviewable decision with a named decision-maker.
- **V-8 Uncertainty in the number itself (CB-S01, CB-S03).** Where the headline figure rests on an assumption the source itself calls approximate, the caveat becomes a rendering requirement, not documentation.
- **V-9 The beneficiary has no organisation (CB-T01–T05).** Products must be single-artefact, physical or one-page, and must not assume a system of record. This is why §3.7 favours cards, charts and one-off documents over software.
- **V-10 The producer has no capability (CB-R04, CB-U05, CB-I02).** Adoption mechanics dominate: a standard applied by hand in a spreadsheet mid-crisis (`U05-R2` adds a row rather than replacing headers) beats a better model requiring tooling.
- **V-11 Discovery vs. expression (CB-R01, CB-R08 vs. CB-R05, CB-R06).** Discovery products optimise for aggregation and minimum mandatory metadata; expression products optimise for encoding nuance. Combining them in S-11 requires that the discovery layer never becomes the ceiling for the expression layer.
- **V-12 Care vs. coordination (CB-Q01 vs. CB-P05).** A data element that changes a clinical decision has a different error cost from one that feeds a monthly indicator; P-092 makes that distinction visible at the point of entry, which no shared data dictionary does by itself.
- **V-13 Maintenance as the primary situation (CB-C01, CB-F02, CB-S03).** When the product's purpose is keeping something working, event history outranks current state (P-085), and repairability outranks initial quality.
- **V-14 Creation under constraint (CB-C03, CB-R02).** Where the maker is also the maintainer (GVCS), modularity and documentation for multiple audiences are functional requirements, not niceties: `C03-R2` and `C03-R3` exist because the same person builds, fixes and teaches.

## 8. ASSUMPTIONS, MISSING INFORMATION, UNRESOLVED MEANINGS, OPEN FEASIBILITY QUESTIONS

**Assumptions recorded**
- ISIC section letters A–U are treated as stable across Rev. 4 and Rev. 5 for case assignment.
- FORD assignment is by the R&D field a case's *knowledge content* belongs to, not by the industry that funds it; this is why standards bodies' engineering specifications land in F2 and research-data specifications in F1/F5.
- CB-T05 (WHO HWT Scheme) is assigned to ISIC T on the reading that a household treating its own drinking water is own-use production of services. A defensible alternative reading places it in ISIC E or Q; I implemented the T reading and did not build the alternatives.
- "Distinct originating projects or organizations" was implemented as distinct *project-level* clusters, with shared parents (WHO, European Commission, MobilityData) recorded rather than merged.
